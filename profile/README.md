## 🐝 Welcome to the Hive

**SourceHive** is a nonprofit that puts open-source excellence in the spotlight. We bring together developers, creators and technologists who love to build, and we help companies get good at open-source business models and digital leadership.

## 🍯 What we do

| Service | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| 🎓 **Open-source training** | Sessions with thought leaders and industry experts on the principles and practices of open source. |
| 📜 **Open-source policy** | Help designing and rolling out open-source policies that fit an organisation's goals and values. |
| 🧭 **Strategic consulting** | Strategies that line up business goals with open-source principles. |
| 🤝 **Community engagement** | Practical ways to work with open-source communities for lasting collaboration and innovation. |
